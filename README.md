# CarboLoom

**AI-Powered Gamified Carbon Footprint Tracking Platform**

CarboLoom is a web-based sustainability platform that encourages environmentally responsible behavior through **carbon footprint tracking**, **gamification**, and **personalized insights**. Users can log daily activities, monitor their environmental impact, compare progress with peers, and receive actionable recommendations to reduce carbon emissions.

The platform combines data-driven analytics with interactive dashboards and educational content to promote long-term behavioral change toward sustainable living.

---

## Live:
https://carboloomapp.vercel.app/

## Key Features

* **Carbon Footprint Tracking** – Calculate daily and weekly carbon emissions from lifestyle activities.
* **Gamification Engine** – Earn points, unlock badges, and complete sustainability challenges.
* **Peer Comparison** – Compare carbon footprints with users in similar age groups and regions.
* **Personalized Recommendations** – Receive eco-friendly suggestions based on activity patterns.
* **Interactive Dashboard** – Visualize carbon trends, achievements, and progress through dynamic charts.
* **Educational Learning Hub** – Improve sustainability awareness with quizzes and informative content.
